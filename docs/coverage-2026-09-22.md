Ticket releases were described as forthcoming. The Flying Finale 2026 page supplied its own booking link, advance-ticket restriction, parking advice and absence of shuttle buses. Those arrangements are not applied to 2027.

The IWM Flying Finale page listed the Red Arrows and RAF Typhoon Display Team. Both now have programme records for 3 October 2026, independently checked on 22 September. IWM's own programme update date of 14 September is retained in the notes as a separate fact.

Old Buckenham's [published 2026 programme](https://www.oldbuckenhamairshow.com/post/the-definitive-list-2026) supports the Spitfire variants and Saturday/Sunday differences recorded in the new appearance. Its [homepage](https://www.oldbuckenhamairshow.com/) supplies the dates, postal address and advice to follow signs from the A11. The historical programme is not proof of actual flight.

Wales Airshow's FAQ confirms free entry without a ticket. Its [travel page](https://www.walesnationalairshow.com/information/travel/) supplies the promenade location and postcode. These are historical visitor arrangements. The new Wales collection explains that no future Welsh date has been added in this batch.

New photographs were not downloaded. Listings reuse existing credited representative photography, and new alt text avoids claiming a picture shows the event or confirms aircraft participation. Map coordinates indicate approximate venue areas, not entrances.

- Old Buckenham 2027 and Wales Airshow 2027: the sources checked here did not establish next-season dates.

Source review does not mean all older records were rechecked. Their original check dates remain unchanged.
